## Rules of the room

- **Claims count.** An assignee, an open linked PR, or a recent
  "I'll take this" comment means someone else got there first. Read
  claim signals exactly as the rubric states them.
- **Claim upstream the moment you commit.** When you decide to take
  an issue, say so on the issue (what you plan to do, roughly when).
- **Follow the repository's own rules.** Its CONTRIBUTING guide, PR
  template, and any AI-use policy outrank anything in this toolkit.
